# Code of Conduct

ErsatzRS is intended to become a public open-source project. Participation is
expected to be respectful, focused, and constructive.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful of different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Discuss technical tradeoffs with evidence and concrete examples.
- Keep feedback focused on the work.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them promptly.
- Respect privacy and do not publish private media paths, logs, credentials, or
  personal information.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or bad-faith argument.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Abuse of project channels for spam, unrelated promotion, or security
  disclosure pressure.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments, close issues, block users, or restrict
participation when behavior harms the project or its contributors.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact listed in the
project's GitHub profile. Security vulnerabilities must use the process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not the conduct process.
